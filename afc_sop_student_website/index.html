Welcome to the AFC SOP Website

This website helps African Foods Company employees find important procedures and understand how to follow them.

Remember: Follow company rules and contact the IT department if you need help.

1. IT Security

  • Use strong passwords and never share them.
  • Use multi-factor authentication when required.
  • Do not open suspicious emails or links.
  • Report lost devices or security problems to IT.
  • Keep your computer and software updated.

2. Acceptable Use Policy

  • Use company computers mainly for work.
  • Do not install software without permission from IT.
  • Keep company and customer information private.
  • Lock your computer when you leave your desk.
  • Report suspicious activity to the Service Desk.

3. Service Desk / Help Desk

Employees should report computer, network, printer and software problems to the Service Desk.

  1. Explain the problem and provide your office location.
  2. Give the technician your device name if you know it.
  3. Write down your ticket number.
  4. Follow the instructions given by IT.
  5. Never give your password or MFA code to anyone.

Priority levels: P1 = critical, P2 = high, P3 = normal, P4 = low.

4. Purchasing Equipment and Software

  1. Explain why the equipment or software is needed.
  2. Ask your department manager for approval.
  3. IT checks that the item is suitable and secure.
  4. Finance checks the budget and supplier quotes.
  5. Wait for approval before placing the order.
  6. IT records new equipment in the asset register.

5. Disaster Recovery

Disaster recovery helps AFC restore systems after an outage, data loss or cyberattack.

  • Report the problem to IT as soon as possible.
  • Do not try to fix serious server problems yourself.
  • Follow IT instructions during an outage.
  • Keep important company files in approved locations.
  • IT must test backups to make sure data can be restored.

6. Network and Systems

This section is mainly for authorised IT staff.

  • Only authorised staff may change network settings.
  • Back up network configurations before making changes.
  • Use approved procedures when updating systems.
  • Keep network equipment secure.
  • Record changes and report network problems.

7. Customer Website Updates

  1. Marketing submits the requested website change.
  2. Operations checks that product details are correct.
  3. The content and images are reviewed before publishing.
  4. The authorised person tests and approves the change.
  5. The update is published and recorded.
  6. A backup is kept in case the change must be reversed.

Need Help?

Contact the AFC Service Desk through the company's approved support channel. Report serious security incidents or major outages immediately.
